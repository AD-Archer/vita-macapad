# PS Vita Macapad

A [Macapad](https://github.com/AD-Archer/Macapad) client that turns a PlayStation Vita into a macro pad for your Mac.

<p align="center">
  <img src="screenshots/dashboard.png" width="480" alt="The dashboard: the time and date, the weather, the song playing on the Mac with its artwork, progress and play controls, and the Mac's CPU, memory, battery and disk.">
  <img src="screenshots/pad.png" width="480" alt="A page of keys: media keys, Mission Control, App Windows, Spotlight, Emoji, Screenshot, Lock, Hide App, Minimize and Full Screen.">
</p>

The first page is a dashboard: the time, the weather, what's playing (with play controls) and how the Mac is doing.

Swipe for pages of keys that run Mac actions, open apps and websites (with their icons), press shortcuts or run
Shortcuts. Make up to 12 pages of 15 keys, right on the Vita.

You'll need [Macapad](https://ad-archer.github.io/Macapad/) running on your Mac, on the same Wi-Fi as the Vita.

## Install

Grab `macapad.vpk` from the [newest release](https://github.com/AD-Archer/vita-macapad/releases/latest), copy it to the
Vita (VitaShell's FTP or USB) and install it from VitaShell. Needs a Vita (or PS TV) running HENkaku/Ensō.
